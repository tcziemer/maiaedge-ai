For years, "network federation" meant a great idea stuck behind two walls: trust and technical complexity. Every operator wanted the upside. Almost no one wanted the integration project.

A new whitepaper from CC Squared, The Network Federation Opportunity, lays out why that calculus is finally changing, and what infrastructure owners stand to gain.

The takeaway that resonates most with what we see every day: federation isn't a defensive play. It's a growth play.

When interconnection becomes simple and fast, the economics flip. Regional fiber providers stop selling commoditized pipes and start delivering high-margin enterprise connectivity. Colocation companies become interconnection hubs. Carriers drive utilization across both their infrastructure and their most valuable connectivity services. And every one of them gets a path to new revenue as the AI market matures: cloud on-ramps, GPU as a service, storage, SaaS.

The unlock is in-network technology paired with a carrier-neutral marketplace, so owners can discover and do business with partners while keeping control of their own customer relationships and full visibility into partner networks.

That's the future we're building carrier infrastructure for at MaiaEdge.

Worth a read if you own network infrastructure and you're thinking about where the next margin comes from.
