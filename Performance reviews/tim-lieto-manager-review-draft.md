## Looking Ahead

The next six months are about converting trust and traction into a repeatable engine. Tim's top priorities should be:

1. **Close the next wave of strategic deals.** MOVI proved we can win. The expectation is now a second and third reference logo in H2, with at least one outside the initial segment to demonstrate the playbook travels.
2. **Rebuild the NA pipeline to support 2027.** Coverage today is thinner than the growth plan requires. Tim should drive his own top-of-funnel discipline (cadence, account focus, qualification rigor) and partner with RevOps to make sure the funnel we are reporting on is the funnel we can actually close. The goal by year-end is a forecastable pipeline that supports the 2027 number with appropriate coverage, not a list of hopeful accounts.
3. **Build the bench.** We are about to hire several reps into NA over the next 18 months. Tim should be actively sourcing, interviewing, and developing a hiring profile now so that when we open requisitions we are not starting from zero. This is leadership work, not recruiting work, and it is on the critical path for the 2027 plan.

The through-line across all three is focus. The most useful thing Tim can do in H2 is be ruthless about where his hours go: on-thesis accounts, the deals most likely to convert, and the hires that unlock leverage. Everything else is a distraction from the bar we are setting.

## Final Thoughts / Overall Summary

Tim met expectations strongly in his first half at MaiaEdge. He landed our first meaningful customer, earned a leadership role on merit, and became a trusted voice in the company on what the field actually needs. Those are the right wins for this stage.

The work ahead is harder than the work behind. Closing the second and third strategic deals while simultaneously rebuilding the funnel and recruiting a bench is a tall order, and it will require Tim to operate as a player-coach more than a closer. His strengths (relationship instinct, commercial judgment, willingness to learn out loud) are well suited to this. The development areas (pipeline depth and discipline, and the muscle of building a team rather than just doing the deals) are real and will need deliberate focus, including more partnership with RevOps on forecast hygiene and a structured approach to hiring.

Net: high confidence in Tim as the right leader for NA. The next six months are about turning that confidence into a repeatable, scalable revenue engine.
