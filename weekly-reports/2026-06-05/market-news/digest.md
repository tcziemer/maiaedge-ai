# MaiaEdge Market News - Week of 2026-05-29 to 2026-06-05

Hey Cooper - here's what moved across our 6 ICPs this week.

## CROSS-ICP THEMES

- **Telecom leadership is migrating into neoclouds.** Lambda named Michel Combes (ex-Sprint, SoftBank International, Brightspeed) CEO and John Donovan (ex-AT&T Communications CEO) Chairman, with Jerry Hunter (ex-Snap COO, early AWS infra) as Vice Chairman of Compute Delivery. When carrier operators run the neoclouds, interconnection and customer ownership become board-level questions, not afterthoughts.
- **The AI dark-fiber land rush keeps accelerating.** LSC lit a 500-mile St. Louis-to-Tulsa route with a hyperscale anchor tenant, and Big Fiber closed $250M from Stonepeak and CDPQ to build dark routes into Bay Area, Hillsboro, and Atlanta AI corridors. Operators are converting dark plant into AI revenue faster than they can light it.
- **NaaS automation is hardening into table stakes.** Cirion launched API-driven on-demand wavelength provisioning in LatAm (Ciena + Carma), Telstra International leaned into autonomous networks for AI-era traffic, and Mplify is wrapping its NaaS APIs in MCP. The build-vs-buy fabric decision sharpens every quarter the carriers automate.
- **PE keeps compounding the infrastructure roll-up** across colo (BlackRock-led $40B Aligned close imminent), fiber (DTCP buying Dark Fiber Group in the Nordics), and the TSD channel (recaps at Avant, Bridgepointe, Telarus).

## COLOCATION

:large_blue_circle: *Power constraints redirect colo investment to secondary markets* (<https://www.datacenterknowledge.com/data-center-construction/new-data-center-developments-june-2026|source>)
Northern Virginia power and permitting ceilings are pushing major colo developers into Columbus, San Antonio, and Boise, where land acquisition and permitting activity is climbing. The 2026 US colo market is now pegged around $72B and still concentrating around Equinix, Digital Realty, QTS, and Iron Mountain.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* Secondary-market builds are interconnection deserts on day one. A colo lighting a Boise or San Antonio site has no on-net fabric and a fresh tenant base that needs instant cloud and carrier reach - the cleanest window for a colo to stand up its own provisioning instead of routing tenants to a third-party fabric where they meet a competitor.

:large_blue_circle: *VivoPower nears AI operator tenant at 41.5MW Mo i Rana colo* (<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001681348/000149315226024744/ex99-1.htm|source>)
VivoPower shortlisted AI operator tenants via formal RFP for its fully operational 41.5MW hydro-powered Norway facility, with a tenant deal targeted to finalize by June 30. Power lands below US$0.035/kWh on 100% renewable hydro.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* A single-AI-tenant Nordic site is the textbook Greenfield-to-operational moment. Cheap sovereign power gets the tenant in the door, but a GPU operator in Mo i Rana still needs deterministic paths back to where its customers and data live - the connectivity gap is the opening, not the megawatts.

:large_blue_circle: *BlackRock-led consortium nears $40B Aligned close* (<https://www.cio.com/article/4073969/blackrocks-40b-data-center-deal-opens-a-new-infrastructure-battle-for-cios.html|source>)
The GIP/MGX/AI Infrastructure Partnership consortium (with Microsoft and Nvidia) is set to close its roughly $40B acquisition of Aligned Data Centers in H1 2026, the largest data center deal on record.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* PE-backed colo at this scale fits the consolidation thesis - acquired platforms usually hit a fabric and provisioning-stack review 6-12 months post-close as the new owners chase margin. That review is exactly when a colo decides whether to own its interconnect or keep renting it.

## FIBER

:large_blue_circle: *LSC lights 500-mile St. Louis-Tulsa dark fiber route with hyperscale anchor* (<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lsc-accelerates-ai-infrastructure-with-500-mile-dark-fiber-route-boosted-by-8-ilas-linking-st-louis-and-tulsa-302694110.html|source>)
Light Source Communications announced a new long-haul route linking St. Louis and Tulsa, 500 miles with 8 in-line amplifiers and a hyperscale tenant already on the route.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* A regional operator winning a hyperscale long-haul anchor is a route-miles-to-revenue story. The hard part is everything past the lit wavelength - the NNIs and Type 2 handoffs to reach the tenant's other sites is where 60-90 day provisioning kills the follow-on deals. That is the reach-extension gap we close.

:large_blue_circle: *Big Fiber closes $250M from Stonepeak and CDPQ for AI dark-fiber buildout* (<https://www.datacenterknowledge.com/infrastructure/big-fiber-financing-signals-ai-s-next-infrastructure-land-rush|source>)
Big Fiber secured $250M from Stonepeak and CDPQ to expand dark fiber into Bay Area, Hillsboro, and Atlanta AI markets.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* Infrastructure-fund money on a dark-fiber operator means an aggressive monetization clock. Lit routes into AI corridors need automated cross-connect and instant provisioning to convert IRUs into recurring services, not 90-day truck-roll cycles - the automate-and-monetize pillar lands hard here.

:large_blue_circle: *DTCP acquires Dark Fiber Group to build Nordic fiber platform* (<https://www.dtcp.capital/news-and-insights/detail/dtcp-acquires-dark-fiber-group-to-build-next-generation-fiber-infrastructure-platform-across-the-nordics/|source>)
DTCP is rolling up Dark Fiber Group as the anchor of a next-generation Nordic fiber infrastructure platform.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* Nordic fiber roll-ups are stitching together fiber islands across borders. The integration pain is the OSS mismatch between acquired networks - provisioning across your own newly-combined plant before you even reach an NNI. That internal reach problem is one Tim Z's international conversations should be naming directly.

## NETWORK OPERATOR

:large_blue_circle: *Cirion launches on-demand NaaS in LatAm with Ciena and Carma* (<https://telecomreseller.com/2026/05/20/cirion-launches-initial-phase-of-on-demand-naas-connectivity-in-latin-america-with-ciena-and-carma/|source>)
Cirion stood up API-based automated wavelength provisioning across its fiber and subsea footprint, integrating Carma with Ciena's Navigator control suite.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* A regional carrier shipping on-demand wavelengths is exactly the automate-then-monetize arc. Carriers that get here organically prove the demand; the ones still hand-provisioning are the conversation - carrier infrastructure that gives them the same instant provisioning without a multi-year platform build.

:large_blue_circle: *Telstra International bets on autonomous networks for the AI era* (<https://www.fierce-network.com/broadband/telstra-international-bets-autonomous-networks-keep-pace-ai-era-demand|source>)
Telstra International is redesigning subsea and terrestrial provisioning around autonomous networks and open APIs (TM Forum, Mplify) as AI reshapes global traffic.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* Tier 1s standardizing on open provisioning APIs validates the whole thesis - the market is moving to programmable interconnect. The smaller operators in our pipeline cannot fund a Telstra-scale automation program, which is precisely the reach-extension and automation gap we fill.

:large_blue_circle: *NTT Data $1B Asian subsea cable kicks off a hectic year for subsea* (<https://www.lightreading.com/cable-technology/ntt-data-s-1b-asian-cable-launches-hectic-year-for-subsea|source>)
NTT Data's $1B Asian cable opens a year forecast to bring roughly 40 new systems and $6B of subsea capacity into service, much of it AI and cloud driven.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* Subsea capacity is landing faster than terrestrial backhaul can absorb it. Every new cable landing station is a fresh interconnection point that needs deterministic onward paths - a structural tailwind for subsea cable operators and the carriers that hand off to them.

## NEOCLOUD

:large_blue_circle: *Lambda names Michel Combes CEO and John Donovan Chairman* (<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/lambda-hires-michel-combes-as-ceo-co-founder-stephen-balaban-shifts-to-cto-role/|source>)
Lambda installed a carrier-grade leadership bench - Combes (ex-Sprint, SoftBank, Brightspeed) as CEO, Donovan (ex-AT&T) as Chairman, Jerry Hunter (ex-Snap, AWS) on compute delivery - to scale toward gigawatt AI infrastructure.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* Telecom operators are now running the neoclouds. Combes and Donovan know exactly what private, deterministic interconnect is worth because they sold it for decades - this is a leadership profile that buys carrier infrastructure thinking, not a best-effort hop. Strong account to re-prioritize.

:large_blue_circle: *IREN signs $3.4B NVIDIA AI Cloud contract* (<https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-and-iren-announce-strategic-partnership-to-accelerate-deployment-of-up-to-5-gigawatts-of-ai-infrastructure|source>)
IREN committed to a 5-year, $3.4B NVIDIA AI Cloud deal for air-cooled Blackwell GPUs across existing capacity at Childress, with broader plans up to 5GW.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* A former bitcoin miner turning megawatts into contracted GPU cloud is the crypto-to-AI pattern playing out at scale. Sites built for mining were never wired for low-latency multi-site inference - the deterministic-paths gap widens the moment the workload shifts from training to agentic inference.

:large_blue_circle: *SoftBank details sovereign GPU cloud on Infrinia AI Cloud OS* (<https://www.rcrwireless.com/20260527/ai/softbank-gpu-ai-cloud|source>)
SoftBank's GB200 NVL72 sovereign GPU cloud (Infrinia OS) opened in beta, with commercial launch in October 2026, internal-first then external. (Context: this account surfaced Cold to Warm in last Monday's signal scan.)
> *MaiaEdge angle:* Sovereign-by-design is the pitch, but sovereignty without provably private routing is marketing. A sovereign cloud serving external customers needs paths it can prove are private - that qualified-sovereignty framing is exactly how we engage without tripping the operator-sovereignty misread.

## MSP / AGGREGATOR

:large_blue_circle: *TSD recapitalization wave accelerates across the channel* (<https://www.channeldive.com/news/5-channel-trends-2026-hyperscaler-tsd-var-msp/809037/|source>)
The PE owners who bought into TSDs in 2020-2021 are positioning for recaps - Pamlico re-upping with Avant, Charlesbank-backed Bridgepointe, Columbia Capital-backed Telarus - as connectivity stays the largest TSD revenue line at a $16.6B market.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* Recap pressure pushes TSDs to differentiate their connectivity line-card beyond reselling the same carriers. An aggregator that can offer automated, brandable provisioning has a margin story to tell a new sponsor - the monetize-and-automate pillar in channel language.

:large_blue_circle: *Telarus pushes Lumen NaaS line-card plus a fiber incentive* (<https://www.telarus.com/about-us/news/|source>)
Telarus ($2.9B gross billings, 11% YoY) is promoting Lumen NaaS earnings potential to partners alongside a limited-time new-business fiber incentive.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* When the largest TSD leads with NaaS, every aggregator behind it needs a connectivity-automation answer. That demand-pull is the wedge - carrier infrastructure that lets the aggregator wholesale its own provisioning rather than pass the customer to a carrier's portal.

## ENTERPRISE (Multi-DC ICP)

:large_blue_circle: *NVIDIA blueprint pulls enterprise verticals into self-run AI infrastructure* (<https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-partners-ai-infrastructure-america|source>)
NVIDIA and partners detailed a US AI-infrastructure blueprint with healthcare and pharmaceutical workloads called out as early adopters building for themselves, not just renting.
> *MaiaEdge angle:* Healthcare Systems enterprises (pharma included) standing up in-house GPU clusters across multiple sites hit the redundant-and-sovereign requirement immediately - regulated data cannot ride best-effort paths between DCs. The multi-DC enterprise with in-house net eng is the exact Enterprise ICP profile, and self-run AI is the trigger.
