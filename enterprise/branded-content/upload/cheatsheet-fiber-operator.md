# Partner Cheat Sheet - Fiber Operator

**MaiaEdge for Fiber Operators**
*Use this when you're calling on regional CLECs, long-haul backbones, dark fiber specialists, or muni/co-op fiber operators.*

---

## Know Your Customer

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| **What They Own** | Physical fiber infrastructure, optical transport. Measure network in route miles. Often fragmented across disconnected fiber islands with different systems at each location. |
| **Revenue Model** | Dark fiber (IRUs), lit wavelengths, metro Ethernet, wholesale services. 30-70% of capacity typically underutilized. |
| **Scale** | 50-2,000 employees, $50M-$1B revenue, regional focus (2-10 state footprint). |
| **Competitive Reality** | NNIs take 60-90 days. Type 2 circuits are visibility black holes. PE-backed operators carry heavy leverage and need monetization velocity. |
| **Why They Buy MaiaEdge** | Light dark fiber as on-demand sellable services. Activate partner paths in minutes. Hop-by-hop visibility across Type 2. Win multi-state deals they're losing today. |

## Special Cases

**Long Haul / Backbone (Zayo, Lumen, Cogent class):** Don't position as automation replacement. They already have it. Lead with cross-carrier extension as a layer above their incumbent automation - partner activation in minutes across operators they already peer with. ("Cross-carrier federation" as a verb form is banned in cold body and LinkedIn body; "Federated Private Networking" as the noun-phrase category descriptor stays canonical for partner-facing collateral.)

**BEAD-funded operators:** BEAD terms apply only to grant-funded strands. MaiaEdge monetizes the commercial IRU and wholesale segments. Two revenue models on the same plant.

**Middle-mile-only / anchor-institution operators:** Structural misfit. Revenue base is IRU + anchor contracts, not on-demand wholesale. Flag and pass.
