## Wave 1 — Craft structure vs current structure (the test that matters)

**Hypothesis:** the craft-voice cold structure (structural truth → craft line → show-me give → call-ask; email-writing-rules.md § Craft Voice) outconverts the post-retrofit signal-opener structure on anchor-less cold lists.

- **List:** next cold wave, one segment (fiber or neocloud), ~300 contacts, Tier 1-3, persona-blocklist + hygiene + Step 1.6 gates on both arms.
- **Arm A (craft):** full craft structure with the live-demo give.
- **Arm B (control):** current structure (signal/Omdia-class opener + call-ask close — the post-retrofit Campaign A shape).
- **n:** ≥150/arm. Same senders, same subjects style per arm, same send windows, Batch Fingerprint Gate on BOTH arms.
- **Read at 14 days:** human-reply rate, positive rate, meetings booked, per-account unsub rate.
- **Decision rule:** at our deal size, 3+ positives in one arm vs 0-1 in the other on n=150 is decision-grade direction; declare a winner only when the direction repeats in Wave 2. (Power honesty: proving +1pt at 95% CI needs ~1,100/arm — we read direction, not significance.)
- **Control baseline also available:** the 4 ACTIVE June campaigns run out unmodified as the old-structure baseline (Cooper, 2026-06-12).
