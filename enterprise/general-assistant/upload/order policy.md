1.) An order is received and considered valid if we can deliver the entire order within 3 months.
2.) If a customer orders more product than we can deliver within 3 months, we must divide the order into 2 or more orders to create at least one valid order.
3.) Commission calculations are done solely on valid orders
4.) 50% of the Commission is earned on receipt of order. 50% on First Payment Received.
5.) When the ENTIRE order is shipped (i.e. LEAVES OUR DOCK)  to the customer (to one, or more destinations) a "Grace Period" starts.
6.) The Grace period is 60 days for a first order, and 30+ days for a subsequent order (See 11 below).
7.) The Grace period is added to the contract term (i.e. the termination date is pushed out to be equal to SHIPMENT DATE + TERM OF AGREEMENT + GRACE PERIOD). Thus a one year deal for a new customer is really a 14 month deal. A one year deal for a second purchase is 13 months.
8.) The First payment will be due on the SHIPMENT DATE + GRACE PERIOD. This defines a customers BILLING DAY OF MONTH (BDOM) for all subsequent orders.
9.) Invoices will go out 30 days prior to Due date. This implies that a first time customer will receive an invoice on the 30th day post shipment, but not be due for 30 additional days. A repeat customer will get an invoice immediately, but essentially get one month free to account for delivery and setup.
10.) Additional orders will be processed exactly as defined above in steps 1-9 but with the following modifications:
·         We will "automatically" modify the SHIPMENT DATE + GRACE PERIOD to exactly equal their current bill day of month by adding 0 to 30 days. Each Customer will have a unique Billing Day of Month. (NOTE: Another option is to always invoice on the 1st of the Month for all customers always).
·         We will send them a single invoice for ALL outstanding contracts on.
·         Contracts will auto renew.
 In a rare case, the VP of Sales can modify the GRACE PERIOD officially at any time to account for complex issues that may arise from time to time.

```md
# MaiaEdge Order Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines MaiaEdge's order validation, invoicing, billing, contract timing, renewal handling, and commission treatment for initial and follow-on orders.

## Policy Statement
MaiaEdge supports the billing and order structure as proposed in the March 27, 2026 email thread. The auto-renew provision is intended to reduce co-terminous contract issues while preserving flexibility to address exceptional customer requests.

## Order Validity
1. An order is considered valid only if MaiaEdge can deliver the entire order within 3 months.
2. If a customer submits an order that cannot be fully delivered within 3 months, the order must be divided into 2 or more orders so that at least one resulting order qualifies as a valid order.
3. Sales commission calculations apply only to valid orders.

## Sales Commission
1. 50% of the commission is earned upon receipt of a valid order.
2. 50% of the commission is earned upon first payment received.
3. If an existing contract is modified solely to align dates with newer orders, commission applies only to the incremental value.

## Shipment And Grace Period
1. The grace period begins when the entire order ships and leaves MaiaEdge's dock, whether shipment is made to one or multiple destinations.
2. For a first order, the grace period is 60 days.
3. For a subsequent order, the grace period is 30 or more days, subject to billing alignment requirements.
4. In rare and complex situations, the VP of Sales may formally modify the grace period.

## Contract Term
1. The grace period is added to the contract term.
2. The contract termination date is calculated as shipment date plus contract term plus grace period.
3. This means a one-year initial customer agreement effectively runs for 14 months.
4. A one-year follow-on purchase typically runs for 13 months, subject to billing alignment.
5. The contract is intended to be in force once the PBC leaves MaiaEdge's dock so the equipment remains covered under the MSA during delivery and setup.

## Billing
1. First payment is due on shipment date plus grace period.
2. This due date establishes the customer's Billing Day of Month (BDOM) for future orders.
3. Invoices are issued 30 days prior to the due date.
4. For a first order, the customer receives an invoice approximately 30 days after shipment and payment is due 30 days later.
5. For a repeat order, the customer may receive an invoice immediately, effectively allowing about one month for delivery and setup before payment is due.

## Follow-On Orders
1. Additional orders follow the same framework as initial orders unless otherwise stated here.
2. For follow-on orders, MaiaEdge will automatically adjust shipment date plus grace period by 0 to 30 days as needed so the due date aligns with the customer's existing BDOM.
3. Each customer will generally have a unique BDOM.
4. MaiaEdge may alternatively choose a standardized invoice date, such as the first day of the month, if adopted operationally.
5. MaiaEdge will issue a single invoice covering all outstanding contracts for that customer.
6. Contracts will auto-renew.

## Auto-Renew And Co-Terminus Handling
1. Auto-renew is intended to mitigate co-terminous contract issues without requiring MaiaEdge to rewrite existing contracts each time a new order is placed.
2. The preferred approach is to consolidate invoicing rather than force all contracts into a single termination date.
3. If a co-terminous issue still arises, MaiaEdge may address it by allowing the customer to purchase the existing systems and extend the date to align with the most recent orders, as needed.
4. Contract extension for co-terminous alignment should be handled only when required or specifically requested by the customer.
5. In general, there is no operational advantage to modifying existing contracts solely to force co-termination.

## Renewal Approach
1. Allowing existing contracts to auto-renew is generally preferable to extending all prior orders each time a customer places an additional order.
2. This approach reduces complexity for both MaiaEdge and the customer.
3. It also avoids requiring a customer to extend a large installed base simply to add a comparatively small number of new units.
4. MaiaEdge may also retain greater pricing flexibility on renewals than on new sales.

## Invoice Transparency
1. Customer invoices should show the contract start date and end date for each line item.
2. This invoice detail serves as notice that the related contract will auto-renew.

## Administrative Notes
1. This policy is designed to consolidate invoicing across multiple purchases while maintaining separate contract timelines where appropriate.
2. The structure is intended to mirror established telecom and fiber billing practices in which customers receive one monthly invoice covering multiple contracted items.

## Approval Basis
This document reflects the policy direction supported in the March 27, 2026 internal email thread, including Tim Ziemer's statement that he reviewed the policy, supports it as written, and agrees that the auto-renew provision addresses the co-terminous issue while preserving a path to align existing systems with the latest orders when needed.
```
