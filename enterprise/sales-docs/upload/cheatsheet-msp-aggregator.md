| **Why They Buy MaiaEdge** | End-to-end visibility across carriers. Match Tier 1 speed without capex. AI-ready upstream they can sell. White-label fabric without becoming an infrastructure operator. |

## ICP Exclusion List (NOT MaiaEdge ICP)

Don't waste cycles on these "aggregators" who pattern-match by name but aren't a fit:

- **Voice termination wholesalers** (iBasis voice, IDT, BICS voice, Intelepeer): voice routing, not data paths
- **SMS / A2P / CPaaS aggregators** (Sinch, Infobip, Twilio, Bandwidth): message routing, not connectivity
- **Cellular IoT MVNOs** (Wireless Logic, Kore, 1NCE, EMnify): mobile packet core, not L2/L3 fixed-line
- **Roaming hubs / IPX providers**: voice/cellular, not data transit
- **eSIM / SIM platform vendors**: not connectivity

If your prospect's vocabulary is voice/SMS/IoT-centric (LCR, ASR, SIP trunking, A2P, IMSI), they're outside MaiaEdge ICP.
